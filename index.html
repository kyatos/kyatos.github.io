> bio for the time being

I am kyatos, a spiritual impulse in motion. I work intimately with spiritual, psychological, and somatic currents to help us create the selves, relationships, and worlds we desire. I write poetry and speculative fiction; make performance sigils and spells; and direct performances which stretch what we understand as theatre. Occasionally, I offer astrology natal chart readings.

My publications include slow dream machine (2020), a self-published poetry collection; u/i (2025), a poetry-art chapbook published by Dakota Books; and Annexe (2026; forthcoming), a pamphlet of speculative flash fiction published by Paper Jam.

I co-/directed Descendants (2026; zone 境); Triumph of Our Tired Eyes (2018; University of Warwick); and Flicker (2015; make/space). I co-/created and performed in Loving Things (2020-21; independent solo) and FUME (2022; directed by Pat Toh).

I was a resident artist for the 2025 AiR Programme at The Godown Arts Centre, Kuala Lumpur and the 2024 Residency at the Intercultural Theatre Institute; a participating artist for Natasha, Singapore Biennale 2022; and a resident writer for Centre 42’s New Scripts Residency 2021.

I recently founded performance group zone (境), after co-founding and serving as Company Lead (2020-2024) of Feelers, a research lab of artists and designers focused on the intersections of art and technology.

I am based in Singapore, have worked in the UK, and am open to international projects and engagements.
