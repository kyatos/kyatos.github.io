I am kyatos, a spiritual impulse in motion. I work intimately with spiritual, psychological, and somatic currents (individual and collective) to help us create the selves, relationships, and worlds we desire. I write poetry and make performance sigils and spells.

My publications include slow dream machine (2020); u/i (2025); and Annexe (2026; forthcoming).

I co-/directed Descendants (2026); Triumph of Our Tired Eyes (2018); and Flicker (2015). I co-/created and performed in Loving Things (2020-21) and FUME (2022).

I was a resident artist for the 2025 AiR Programme at The Godown Arts Centre, Kuala Lumpur and the 2024 Residency at the Intercultural Theatre Institute; a participating artist for Natasha, Singapore Biennale 2022; and a resident writer for Centre 42’s New Scripts Residency 2021.

I recently founded performance group zone (境), after co-founding and serving as Company Lead (2020-2024) of Feelers, a research lab of artists and designers focused on the intersections of art and technology.
