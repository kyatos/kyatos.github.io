now

last updated 09/07/2026

I'm finding restful moments amidst accelerating responsibilities: Descendants entering its rehearsal phase with greater dramaturgical and design demands; Opening Acts in its selection process; a number of literary projects coming into their developmental and revision phases.

I'm doing my best to keep up with Odissi classes, boxing, and working up to adding a run or extra workout in ever so often. My body is getting stronger, slowly, and I aim to keep pace with it.

This season has also brought in joyful, loving surprises. I am in love. My relationship with play and pleasure is expanding. The sense of curiosity I felt about being a woman just a few years ago has threaded like a river into the ocean, becoming an invisible line in a much larger and unconfinable body. I continue my affair with femininity; I think we might have something lifelong between us.

I'm also renewing my all-in-one workspace/ bedroom with secondhand furniture and a new layout. I've had this desk since I was in primary school, I think. Imagine that. All these years I've held back, letting my fear of waste outweigh my need for change. I'm biting the bullet now, and committing to some furniture that I hope I'll use for life.

It's queer and divine how everything can shift in an instant. One gaze and suddenly I know. One decision and everything follows that mystery, shifting in choreography and arrangement to flow with it.
