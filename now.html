now

last updated 18/08/2026

This eclipse season has been a spiky, abrasive one. I'm someone who rarely allows their pain to show; this is a gift and a challenge. The magic strand through the pain has been experiencing wisdom come through me, teasing out and altering my egoic choices. To nurture openness, honour withdrawal & retreat, and respond with awareness & willingness-- rather than trying to 'be better' by attempting to pre-empt and have an SOP for everything. Softening into this shape is nothing short of a miracle, a grounding step-stone in the river. Being in the here and now is not conceptual or a compartmentalized practice or discipline; it is a chosen and embodied orientation that permeates reality.

Concretely: the first cycle of Opening Acts has concluded with great exhaustion and satisfaction; work continues for Descendants; editorial projects are in motion. These few days have been hazy due to fatigue, so I've been taking as much of a break as possible, and using that time to re-orient the frazzled parts of my mind, to allow them space to express as well as to be entirely transformed.

I'm beginning to make some longer-term plans in terms of projects for 2027. New artistic impulses have also been emerging-- I have started to lay ground for them. Amidst the fatigue, a stab of clarity marks a faraway point: I am still hurtling towards it, learning to enjoy the glide and glitch as wind interferes, making the path less direct, more revealing.
